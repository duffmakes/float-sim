Heading β

β = direction the waves travel, relative to the model's +X axis: 180° head seas, 90° beam seas, 0° following.

Wave & hydrodynamic settings

Spreading 0 gives long-crested waves; s > 0 applies cos2s short-crested spreading.

Added mass and damping are estimates (no diffraction/radiation solver). Defaults: strip-theory semicircle for heave, 0.2·I for roll, damping as a fraction of critical.

Long-wave (G.I. Taylor) correction: adds added mass × the incident acceleration averaged over the submerged volume. In the trade study (docs/rao-trade-study.md) it fixes the 50 % low horizontal force of Froude–Krylov (spar within 5 % of MacCamy–Fuchs). For heave it helps in long waves but overcorrects near resonance unless A33 is calibrated, so it is off by default.

9 · Drop / rise test

Release the body from a chosen height to watch it fall into the water, plunge and recover, or rise from below the surface and settle. Uses the mass, CoG, inertia and orientation above. It does not affect the RAO or sea-state tools, which are locked while the body is placed away from its float position.
